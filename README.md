#Indian  sales Store ( Interactive Dashboard Creation using MS excel )
## Project Objective 
Analyze overall sales and profit performance of the store.
Identify top-performing products, categories, and regions.
Track monthly and yearly sales trends.
Compare performance across customer segments and states.
Monitor shipping efficiency and order patterns.
Present data through an interactive and easy-to-understand dashboard for better business decision-making.
## Dataset used 
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/abuhumzakhan/store-data
## Questions (KPIs) 
What is the total sales revenue generated from 2019 to 2023?
What is the total profit earned during the period?
How many orders were placed?
What is the average sales value per order?
Which product category generated the highest sales?
Which category contributed the highest profit?
What are the top 10 best-selling products?
